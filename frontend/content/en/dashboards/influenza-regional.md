### Notes on the data

- The data comes from the **National Reference Laboratory for Influenza and Non-influenza Respiratory Viruses** at NIPH Prague
- It covers all laboratory-confirmed respiratory viruses (Influenza A/B, RSV, rhinoviruses, SARS-CoV-2, etc.)
- Category: **virus detection** (PCR/immunofluorescence)
- Years 2022–2024: cumulative totals from the last weekly PDF of the season
- Season 2024/25: weekly data from NRL reports (week 40/2024 – week 36/2025)
- Regions with fewer detections (e.g. the Karlovy Vary Region) may also reflect lower NRL reporting capacity
