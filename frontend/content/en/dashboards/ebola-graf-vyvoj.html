How to read the chart: this is the trajectory of reported confirmed cases and deaths among confirmed cases in the DRC, not when the infections actually occurred. INSP repeatedly points out that the DHIS2 database is being cleaned and harmonised; daily changes may therefore include retrospective laboratory confirmation or revised case classification.

Chart data cut-off: the series ends with the latest public detailed report, SitRep 110 with data as of 1 September. SitRep 110 has been added without reconstructing missing days. See Current situation for the latest overview.

Sources and limitations

The series is built from the official SitReps of the DRC INSP/COUSP for the current 17th epidemic of Ebola disease in the DRC (in the original MVB, maladie à virus Bundibugyo). Uganda is not included in the daily series because its public dashboard does not provide a historical daily series traceable in the same way. The dashboard as of 28 July separately reports 20 confirmed cases, 2 deaths, 18 recovered, 0 current admissions, 0 active contacts under follow-up and 821 contacts with completed 21-day follow-up.

SitReps 86–110 state their daily values directly. SitRep 89 reports 118 cases while the cumulative total rises by 117 because of ongoing harmonisation; SitRep 93 reports 101 cases while the cumulative total rises by 102 because one earlier case had been omitted. SitReps 107–110 directly report case/death pairs of 96/49, 59/39, 86/57 and 64/32; death values sum community deaths and confirmed deaths in treatment centres. The number of affected health zones remains 60. The bars therefore use directly reported daily values rather than differences between cumulative totals. Older source limitations and discrepancies are documented in detail in the accompanying README.

Reference sources: DRC INSP/COUSP SitRep 110, SitRep 109, SitRep 108, ECDC outbreak page, Uganda Ministry of Health dashboard and WHO daily epidemiological update 20260903. Individual SitRep links are listed in the table.
