### What hantavirus is and why this outbreak is exceptional

**Hantaviruses** are RNA viruses transmitted by rodents (through the air and by contact with droppings). They cause two serious clinical syndromes:
- **HFRS** — haemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome (Europe, Asia)
- **HPS/HCPS** — hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome (the Americas), 30–40% case fatality

**Andes virus** (*Orthohantavirus andesense*) is the only hantavirus with demonstrated **human-to-human transmission** — epidemiologically exceptional. All other hantaviruses are transmitted only from rodents.

---

### The 2026 outbreak — MV Hondius cruise ship

In 2026, **community transmission of Andes virus** linked to the MV Hondius cruise ship was identified. As of June 2026:

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Confirmed cases | 12 |
| Probable cases | 1 |
| Deaths | 3 |
| Contacts under follow-up | 600+ |
| Countries/territories affected | 32 |
| Rt (reproduction number) | 0.7 (declining trend) |

The **incubation period** of Andes virus is 4–42 days; a 42-day quarantine of contacts is recommended.

---

### Genomics and phylogenetics

The S, M and L segments of the virus have been sequenced. The phylogeny places this strain among the Argentinian Andes virus strains — consistent with the ship's route through South America.

Genomic data is available at: [Pathoplexus](https://pathoplexus.org) · [virological.org](https://virological.org)

---

### Risk to the Czech Republic

The Czech Republic is not an endemic area for Andes virus. The risk of an imported case is **low**, but it cannot be ruled out for travellers from Argentina or Chile. The domestic **Puumala** hantavirus (carried by the bank vole) causes HFRS with a mild course and does occur in the Czech Republic.
