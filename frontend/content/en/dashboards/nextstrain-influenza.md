Nextstrain maintains continuously updated phylogenetic trees for **seasonal influenza** — a key tool for tracking antigenic drift and selecting strains for vaccines.

### What the tree shows

- **Antigenic clades** — colour-coded groups sharing a similar antigen
- **Vaccine strains** — the WHO-recommended strains for the season are marked
- **Geographic distribution** — where each sequence comes from
- **HA mutations** — amino-acid substitutions mapped onto the tree
