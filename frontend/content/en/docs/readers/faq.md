{{< faq q="Is Pathogen Portal CZ an official source of data?" >}}
No. The portal takes over and processes open data from public institutions. For public health
decisions, the data and statements of the Ministry of Health, SZÚ, ÚZIS and the regional public
health authorities are authoritative.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq q="Why does a number on the portal differ from the one published by the Ministry of Health, SZÚ or ÚZIS?" >}}
The most common reasons: (1) the data were downloaded at a different moment and the source has since
added or corrected reports; (2) the portal aggregates the data differently, for example by week
instead of by day; (3) it is a derived value (incidence, positivity), not the original one. If none of
these explains the difference, [report an error](/en/about/data/#report-an-error).
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq q="How often are the data updated?" >}}
It depends on the source: some daily, others weekly or once a year. An overview is on the page
[Data freshness](/en/docs/readers/data-freshness/).
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq q="Why do Signals show an old month?" >}}
They are based on the open ISIN data export, which currently ends in December 2025. Later months will
be evaluated once ÚZIS publishes further data.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq q="May I use the data and charts in a publication or presentation?" >}}
Yes. The portal's texts and processed data are under the CC BY 4.0 licence; data taken over from
elsewhere are under the licence of the original source. Details are on the page
[Downloads and licences](/en/docs/readers/downloads-and-licences/).
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq q="Why are Prague and the Central Bohemian Region combined for influenza?" >}}
They share virological laboratories, so they report together.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq q="Can I find data on individual patients on the portal?" >}}
No. The portal processes only public aggregated data without personal data and does not provide
access to non-public data.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq q=`What does the "AI-assisted" badge mean?` >}}
The text was prepared from publicly available sources with the help of AI tools and reviewed by an
expert before publication. The data-origin badges are explained on the
[About](/en/about/#where-the-data-comes-from) page.
{{< /faq >}}
