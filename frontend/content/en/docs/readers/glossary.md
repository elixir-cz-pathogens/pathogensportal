## Indicators and methods

| Term | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **ARI**, acute respiratory infection | A clinical diagnosis: sudden onset and at least one respiratory symptom (cough, sore throat, shortness of breath, runny nose). It covers infections caused by various viruses and is not laboratory-confirmed. |
| **ILI**, influenza-like illness | A clinical diagnosis by the EU case definition: sudden onset, at least one systemic symptom (fever, malaise, headache, muscle pain) and at least one respiratory symptom (cough, sore throat, shortness of breath). ILI is a subset of ARI. |
| **Reported case** | A case notified to the mandatory reporting system. The number of reported cases is usually lower than the true number of infections and depends on testing and on whether the ill see a doctor. |
| **Laboratory detection** | A positive laboratory test of a specimen for a given pathogen. The number of detections depends on the number of specimens tested. |
| **Positivity** | The share of positive results among the specimens tested, in per cent. It depends less on testing volume than the number of detections. |
| **Incidence per 100,000 population** | The number of new cases in a period per 100,000 population. The denominator is the population according to the Czech Statistical Office. |
| **7-day incidence** | The number of new cases in the last 7 days per 100,000 population. |
| **CFR**, case fatality ratio | The share of deaths among confirmed cases. It is overestimated when testing is weak. |
| **IFR**, infection fatality ratio | The share of deaths among all infected people, including those not detected. It is lower than the CFR. |
| **Calendar week** | A week by ISO 8601, Monday to Sunday. Week 1 contains the first Thursday of the year. "Week 36/26" means the 36th week of 2026. |
| **Influenza season** | The period from calendar week 40 (turn of September and October) to week 20 of the following year (mid-May). |
| **MEM**, Moving Epidemic Method | A method that derives the epidemic threshold and intensity levels from past seasons. Used by ECDC and WHO. |
| **Epidemic threshold** | The value above which a week counts as epidemic. On the portal it is the result of the MEM method, not the official threshold for declaring an epidemic. |
| **Intensity levels** | Low, medium, high and very high season intensity. The limits are derived from the peaks of past epidemics (the 40th, 90th and 97.5th percentiles). |
| **Expected (endemic) level** | The number of cases the model expects for a given disease, region and month from history and seasonality. |
| **Signal** | A month in which the number of reported cases exceeded the upper limit of normal variation (the 99th percentile of the prediction interval). A signal is not a confirmed outbreak. |
| **Exceedance score** | (reported − expected) / (limit − expected). A value of 1 means a count exactly at the limit. |
| **WIS**, weighted interval score | A measure of the accuracy of probabilistic forecasts. It penalises wide intervals as well as values outside the interval; lower is better. |
| **Quantile** | The value below which a given share of a distribution lies (the median is the 50% quantile). |
| **Phylogenetic tree** | A diagram of the relatedness of pathogen sequences. It shows how variants evolved and spread. |
| **Antigenic drift** | Gradual changes in the surface antigens of a virus (in influenza, haemagglutinin and neuraminidase), which is why the vaccine composition is updated every year. |
| **Wastewater surveillance (WBE)** | Monitoring the genetic material of pathogens in wastewater from treatment plants. An indicator of circulation in the population that does not depend on testing individuals. |

## Data sources and institutions

| Abbreviation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ISIN** | Information System of Infectious Diseases: the national system of mandatory notification of infectious diseases under Act No. 258/2000 Coll., run by ÚZIS. |
| **ICD-10** (Czech: MKN-10) | International Classification of Diseases, 10th revision. |
| **ÚZIS** | Institute of Health Information and Statistics of the Czech Republic. |
| **SZÚ** | National Institute of Public Health. |
| **NRL** | National Reference Laboratory; here the NRL for influenza and non-influenza respiratory viral diseases at SZÚ. |
| **MZČR** | Ministry of Health of the Czech Republic. |
| **KHS** | Regional public health authority. |
| **ČSÚ** | Czech Statistical Office (source of population figures). |
| **ECDC** | European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control. |
| **ERVISS** | European Respiratory Virus Surveillance Summary: joint respiratory surveillance of ECDC and WHO Europe. |
| **WHO FluNet / FluID** | WHO global databases for virological (FluNet) and epidemiological (FluID) influenza data. |
| **RespiCast** | The European platform for forecasts of respiratory diseases (ECDC). |
| **GISAID** | A database for sharing influenza and SARS-CoV-2 sequences. |
| **NUTS 3** | A statistical territorial unit; in the Czech Republic it corresponds to the regions. |
