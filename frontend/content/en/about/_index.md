## About the portal

**Pathogen Portal CZ** is a central gateway to data on the occurrence of infectious
diseases in the Czech Republic — clinical, environmental, and genomic surveillance in
one place. It serves researchers, public health professionals and the public.

## What you will find here

- **Dashboards for individual pathogens** — COVID-19, influenza and ARI, notifiable
  infectious diseases from ISIN, wastewater surveillance, phylogenetics, and situation
  reports for active outbreaks.
- **Signals** — automated detection of exceedances above the expected level across more
  than 1,200 time series of notified diseases. A statistical screen, not a confirmed
  outbreak.
- **Datasets and analyses** the charts are built from.

## Where the data comes from

Every dashboard carries a badge showing the origin of its data, because it differs
substantially from page to page:

| Badge | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Public source** | Data published by an external institution (Czech MoH, ÚZIS, NIPH, ECDC, Czech Statistical Office). The portal collects and visualises it; the original source is authoritative. |
| **Our data** | Produced by us, or handed to us for processing. We stand behind it. |
| **AI-processed** | Collected from public sources and written by an AI agent, reviewed by a subject-matter expert before publication. |

## Who runs the portal

The portal is being developed as part of the **ELIXIR IMPACT2** project, supported by the
[Swiss-Czech Cooperation Programme](https://www.swiss-contribution.cz/), and runs on
[CESNET](https://www.cesnet.cz) infrastructure.

The full list of collaborating institutions is on the **Partners** page; the people
behind it are listed under **People**.
