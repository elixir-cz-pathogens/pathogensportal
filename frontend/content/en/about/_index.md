## About the portal

**Pathogen Portal CZ** is a central gateway to data on the occurrence of infectious diseases in the Czech Republic — clinical, environmental, and genomic surveillance in one place.

The portal is being developed as part of the **ELIXIR IMPACT2** project, supported by the [Swiss-Czech Cooperation Programme](https://www.swiss-contribution.cz/), and runs on [CESNET](https://www.cesnet.cz) infrastructure.
