## About the portal

**Pathogen Portal CZ** is a central gateway to data on the occurrence of infectious diseases in the Czech Republic — clinical, environmental, and genomic surveillance in one place.

The portal is being developed as part of the Czech–Swiss **ELIXIR IMPACT2** project and runs on [CESNET](https://www.cesnet.cz) infrastructure.

Technical contact for the portal administrator: **Jan Pačes, Ph.D.** — [hpaces@img.cas.cz](mailto:hpaces@img.cas.cz)
