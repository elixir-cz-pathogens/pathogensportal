**Pathogen Portal CZ** brings together in one place data on the occurrence of infectious diseases in the Czech Republic: reported cases, laboratory test results, wastewater measurements and genetic analyses of pathogens. It serves researchers, public health professionals and the public.

## What you will find here

- **Dashboards on various topics:** COVID-19, influenza and ARI, notifiable infectious
  diseases from ISIN, wastewater, phylogenetics and situation reports on active outbreaks.
- **Signals:** automatic detection of months in which the number of reported cases clearly
  exceeded the expected level (over 1,200 time series by diagnosis and region). A signal is
  not a confirmed outbreak.
- **Data downloads:** the processed data files the charts are drawn from (see
  [Data and licences](/about/data/)).

## Where the data comes from

Every dashboard carries a badge showing the origin of its data:

| Badge | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Public source** | Data published by an external institution (Czech MoH, ÚZIS, NIPH, ECDC, Czech Statistical Office). The portal takes it over and visualises it; the original source is authoritative. |
| **Own analysis** | Calculations and analyses by the portal operator (e.g. Signals, influenza intensity thresholds). The operator is responsible for the method and processing; the source of the input data is given on the dashboard. |
| **AI-assisted** | Text prepared from publicly available sources with the help of AI tools and reviewed by an expert before publication. |

## Who runs the portal

The portal is operated by the
[Institute of Molecular Genetics of the Czech Academy of Sciences](https://www.img.cas.cz) (IMG),
which is responsible for it.

The portal is being developed as part of the **ELIXIR IMPACT2** project, supported by the
[Swiss-Czech Cooperation Programme](https://www.swiss-contribution.cz/), and runs on
[CESNET](https://www.cesnet.cz) infrastructure with the support of our [partners](/about/affiliations/).
