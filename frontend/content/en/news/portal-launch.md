**Pathogen Portal CZ** is an open platform for infectious disease surveillance data in the Czech Republic.
The portal brings together clinical, genomic, and environmental surveillance data in one place.

Dashboards are currently available for SARS-CoV-2, influenza, infectious diseases reported through ISIN, and wastewater monitoring.
